# What's New With 4.10.0

CBOrm 4.10.0 focuses on Adobe ColdFusion 2025 support and enhanced BoxLang compatibility.

## Added

### Platform Support

* **Adobe ColdFusion 2025 Certification**: Full support and testing for Adobe ColdFusion 2025
* **BoxLang BE Tests**: Enhanced test coverage for BoxLang Bleeding Edge builds

## Fixed

### Performance & Concurrency

* **Restrictions Concurrency Fix**: Resolved a critical concurrency issue where restrictions could overlap under high-load scenarios, ensuring thread-safe operation in production environments

## Release Date

June 3, 2025
